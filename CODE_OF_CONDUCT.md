# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), v2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Smith Maker a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Being honest about gaps and uncertainty (a documented gap beats a confident wrong answer)
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the work

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions —
and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers by opening a **private vulnerability/conduct report**
through this repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, which is
confidential. Maintainers will review and respond as promptly as they are able,
and will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
